Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are. My Pet Book (دفتر أليفي) is an app made by Mohamed Bassiuony in Alexandria, Egypt. Contact: mohamedbassiuony99@gmail.com.

The short version. Your pets' book lives on your phone. Information leaves your phone only when you use a feature that needs it, and only what that feature needs. We do not sell data, we show no ads, and we use no advertising or tracking tools.

Not medical advice. My Pet Book keeps records. It does not give dosage or treatment advice, and it does not recommend vets outside the app. Always ask a vet before you give your pet any medicine, vaccine or treatment.

1. What stays on your phone

Your pets, health records, photos, notes, weights, your name and your settings are stored on your phone. We cannot see them. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, and their text is never sent to us.

3. Who processes data for us

Supabase (database and server functions), Anthropic and Google (reading scanned photos), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Apple and Google (sign-in and payments), Google Workspace (sign-in code emails). Some of these services store or process data outside Egypt.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Export a pet's book as PDF or Excel, or a full backup, from Settings.
  • Sign out of every other phone at once: Settings, Security.
  • Delete your account and everything on our server tied to it: Settings, Delete my account. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, by email.

5. Children

My Pet Book is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Every table on our server has row level security, so people can change only their own data. No system is perfectly safe; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

7. Changes

When the app changes what it does with data, we update this page and the date at the top.
